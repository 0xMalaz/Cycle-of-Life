Cycle of Life

Atoms are constantly constructing DNAs, spending their lifetime, and finally, vanish only to be replaced by new atoms to complete the cycle of life.

This time every strand carries a genome. It eats atoms to build itself, finds a partner and has children who inherit a mix of both, with the odd mutation. When it dies it unzips, and every atom goes back into the soup. Nothing is ever lost.
